## Overview

After completing my thesis in May 2024, a friend and I completed a 2 month bike packing trip through Malawi, Tanzania and into Kenya. We wanted to experience travel in the most honest method possible. Just us, the bikes and our legs pedalling us into the unknown. 

It's a hard task summarising the two months, but the strongest emotion was: 'it goes!' It was thrilling that _we did it_. We actually managed? Of course we were often out of our comfort zones but always somehow felt we could handle it, we also learnt so much about East Africa - the people and customs, the animals and landscapes, and how it is to do a bikepacking tour of this length. But maybe the biggest takeaway for us, is our new found respect for the confusion we felt being a guest in a land so harshly impacted by colonisation. The feeling of being privileged - us students, pedalling away on our nice bicycles, with our air mattresses and ultralight cooking pots, going for a little adventure... It is always hard to reconcile the romantic image in one's head with the harshness of gritty travel. 

We loved the heat and the dust and the dozens of people that welcomed us into their homes and hearts, the burning, red sunsets and the wide open spaces, the brilliant, starry night-skies, the rituals of setting up a tent every evening next to a mud hut or two, or of cooking food over a fire, and of learning that we are mentally and physically strong enough for a trip like this.
