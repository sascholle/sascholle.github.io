abstract: "Latent Windhose: Mechanistic Analysis of GraphCast Latent Space. Linear Analysis of the Internal Activations in a Data-Driven Weather Model"
summary: 'Abstract: Data-driven weather forecasting models such as GraphCast have achieved state-of-the-art predictive skill with lower computational costs than traditional numerical weather prediction systems. Despite this success, an open question is whether these models learn physically meaningful representations of atmospheric dynamics or primarily exploit statistical correlations in the training data. In this work, we build on current research showing the presence of interpretable physical features in GraphCast’s latent space by investigating how much of its activation space can be explained using purely linear methods, produce a systematic mapping between latent features to physical features and examine forecast response to perturbed latent directions.'
